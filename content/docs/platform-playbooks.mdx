A playbook tells your agent how to act, or what it needs to know, on your website. The 3Guide team writes and maintains them, so you get an expert agent without writing prompts.

Some playbooks give the agent a **role**:

- **Sales Agent**: qualify the visitor, recommend a plan and lead them to the next step in your app
- **Support Agent**: diagnose the problem, fix it with the user and escalate cleanly when it can't

Others give it **know-how** about a product or field, such as *System Architecture on AWS* or *Google Cloud Console Navigation*. They're grouped as Agents, Cloud, Engineering, Games, Platform and Support.

**Why it matters:** your agent behaves like a trained salesperson, support rep or specialist from day one. Playbooks are kept up to date for you, and a playbook that's switched on adds almost nothing to each conversation until a question actually needs it.

Open them from **Automation → Platform Playbooks**.

## Use a playbook

<Steps>
<Step>

### Switch it on

Nothing is on until you switch it on. Search or filter by category, and use the switch on the card. Your plan sets how many can be on at once, and the count is shown at the top right.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose where it runs

Under **Runs in**, pick the modes that use it:

| Mode | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **All modes** | Everywhere |
| **Guidance** / **Assistant** | The chat widget's two modes |
| **Extension** | The [browser extension](/docs/install-extension) |
| **Sales agent** / **Support agent** | Those agents in [Agent Blueprint](/docs/agent-blueprint) |
| **Show me** | The **Show me** button on a walkthrough |
| **Coach** | The [Guidance coach](/docs/agent-blueprint#guidance-coach) |

The **Show me** button uses every playbook you've switched on for that product, whatever its modes. To keep a playbook out of conversations, assign it to **Show me** only.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check when it's used

Each card lists keywords (for example *pricing, plan, trial* for Sales Agent) that bring the playbook into a conversation. Some apply only on certain sites, shown as **Applies on**, for example `console.aws.amazon.com`.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Need something different?

You can't edit a playbook's text, but you can ask for changes:

- **Not good enough?** on a card tells the 3Guide team that playbook needs improving.
- **Request a skill** at the top of the page asks for a new playbook. Describe what the agent should do.
- In [Agent Blueprint](/docs/agent-blueprint), a **Requested behaviour** block does the same from inside your flow.

A person on the 3Guide team writes each requested playbook.
