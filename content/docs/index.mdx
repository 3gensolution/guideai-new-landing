3Guide has four products. Each works on its own, and they share one account, one knowledge base of your app, and one plan.

| Product | What it does | Where you set it up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guidance** | Step-by-step guides, help hints and announcements on the live page, plus a chat that builds walkthroughs on request | Dashboard → **Guides**, **Help Hints**, **Announcements** |
| **AI Assistant** | Does the task for the user by calling your APIs, and answers from your documents | Dashboard → **Copilot Tools** |
| **Guide Pro** | Interactive demos for your website, sales and training, plus video and written how-to exports | Dashboard → **Guide Pro** + the Chrome extension |
| **Analytics** | Product analytics, session replays, errors, feature flags and surveys, with problems detected for you | Dashboard → **Overview**, **Product**, **Web**, **Replays** |

## What you'll need

- A 3Guide account. Sign up free at [dashboard.3guideai.com](https://dashboard.3guideai.com/signup).
- For your own app: access to its HTML or code, to add one script tag.
- For anything recorded or scanned in the browser: Google Chrome and the [3Guide extension](/docs/install-extension).
