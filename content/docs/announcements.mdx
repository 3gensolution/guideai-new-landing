---
title: Publish announcements
description: Tell users about new features and changes inside the product, where they'll actually see them.
icon: Megaphone
---

Announcements are in-app messages: a modal, a banner or a side drawer. Use them for new features, changes, maintenance notices or offers. Unread announcements also collect behind a small **beacon** with an unread count, so users can catch up later.

**Before you start:** 3Guide is [installed in your app](/docs/quickstart).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the announcement

Open **Content → Announcements** and either **Start from a template** or create a blank one. It opens in the **Announcement Studio**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write and design it

Edit the content on the **Canvas**, then choose **Display as**: a modal in the centre of the screen, a banner across the top (default height 150px, maximum 300px), or a drawer from the side.

Enter a **Preview URL** to see it on top of your real app while you edit.

</Step>
<Step>

### Decide who sees it, and when

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **How to show** | **Auto-show**: appears on its own, after an optional **Auto-show delay**. **Beacon click**: waits in the beacon until the user opens it. |
| **How often** | **Once**, **Per session**, **Per day** or **Every visit** |
| **Route (optional)** | Only show it on matching pages, for example `/billing/*` |
| **Schedule window** | Only show it between two dates, for launches and time-limited offers |
| **Attach guide (optional)** | Add a button that starts one of your [guides](/docs/guides), so "we've launched X" leads straight into "here's how to use X" |

</Step>
<Step>

### Make it live

Switch it to **Active**. Open your app to check it appears where and when you expect.

</Step>
</Steps>

## See how it's doing

The **Announcements** page shows total impressions, average conversion, active triggers and user sentiment for your announcements. Open any announcement again to edit it, or switch it off.

## Defaults for all announcements

Your snippet can set the site-wide default surface, for example `data-announcement-surface="banner"`. Anything you choose in the Studio for a specific announcement takes priority. All options are in the [SDK reference](/docs/sdk-reference#announcements).
