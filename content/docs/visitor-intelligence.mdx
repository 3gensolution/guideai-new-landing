Visitor Intelligence turns anonymous traffic into a list of the **companies** visiting your site, what they looked at, and how often they came back. You can also choose to look up the **people** who gave your site a work email.

**Why it matters:** your sales team can see which companies are interested, and what they care about, before anyone fills in a form. They can follow up with the right message at the right time.

**Before you start:** the [Analytics script](/docs/analytics) is installed. Visitor Intelligence works from the visits it records, so it needs the Analytics script as well as the Guidance script.

Open it from **Visitors** in the dashboard.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Run a scan

On the **Overview** tab, start a scan. 3Guide reads the networks your visitors came from, finds the company websites behind them and cross-checks the evidence. A scan takes about two hours, and your plan sets how many you can run each month.

</Step>
<Step>

### See which companies visited

The **Organisations** tab lists each company the scan confirmed, with a **Strong**, **Partial** or **Weak** match and the evidence behind it, such as the company network or the domain of a work email. Open a company to see its sessions, top pages, location context and how it found you.

Most visits come from home and mobile networks. These stay anonymous and aren't
matched to anyone. A carrier or ISP is shown as a network, not as the visitor's
employer. An organisation is identified only when the evidence supports that
conclusion.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask questions

The **Questions** tab answers common ones for you, such as *Which companies visited?*, *Where did visitors come from?*, *Which entry pages drew the most visitors?* and *Which visitors returned more than three times?*. You can also ask Visitor Intelligence your own question.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Optional: look up people

**Person lookups** are off by default. When you switch them on, the next scan looks up everyone who gave your site a **work email**, for example by signing in or by telling the chat. Their profile shows their name, role, seniority, company, LinkedIn and other public profiles.

- People are only looked up from work emails they gave your site. Disposable addresses are skipped.
- Add your own **Apollo.io** key in **Settings → Integrations** to improve the matches.
- You can refresh or delete a profile at any time, or delete them all.

<Callout type="warn" title="Privacy">
  Person lookups process personal data. Turn them on only if your privacy policy covers it and you have a lawful basis (for example under the GDPR or Nigeria's NDPA). Company-level results don't identify individuals.
</Callout>

Network and location context describes where traffic came from; it does not
identify a person. Home, mobile and other shared networks remain anonymous even
when a city or country is available.
